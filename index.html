About Conscious Consumer

Conscious Consumer is a civic transparency platform inspired by the historic Green Book, which guided Black travelers to safe businesses during the Jim Crow era.

This app helps LGBTQ+ travelers, people of color, religious minorities, people with disabilities, and women make informed decisions about where they spend their money and where they travel.
