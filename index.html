About Conscious Consumer

A civic transparency platform inspired by the historic Green Book, which guided Black travelers to safe businesses during the Jim Crow era. We help LGBTQ+ travelers, people of color, religious minorities, people with disabilities, and women make informed decisions about where they spend their money and where they travel.

Our Mission

To democratize access to corporate transparency data and travel safety information for marginalized communities — putting the power of information in the hands of consumers.

How Scores Work

Businesses start at 100 and lose points based on political donations, negative news coverage, federal court cases, ESG ratings, human rights records, and police violence data.

Travel Safety

State safety ratings draw from NAACP travel advisories, HRC Corporate Equality Index, ACLU legislative tracking, Movement Advancement Project data, and ADA compliance records.

Police Violence Data

Incident data comes from Mapping Police Violence, which tracks all known police killings in the US. Accountability rates reflect criminal charges filed against officers.
